# Photographic materials and lighting

All source assets below are CC0 from Poly Haven, downloaded from its official asset API. The app redistributes 1K color, OpenGL normal and roughness maps converted to WebP, plus a 1K HDR environment. No external requests are needed during play.

- Castle Wall Slates — Rob Tuytel: https://polyhaven.com/a/castle_wall_slates
- Medieval Wood — Rob Tuytel: https://polyhaven.com/a/medieval_wood
- Kloppenheim 06 Pure Sky: https://polyhaven.com/a/kloppenheim_06_puresky
- License: https://polyhaven.com/license

Castle and ship meshes, the courtyard puzzle, characters, original insults and replies, and all music are authored for this game. Monkey Island is a reference for the verbal sword-duel mechanic and adventurous tone; no music, dialogue or characters from it are included.

RGBELoader.js and three.module.js: Three.js r160, MIT; see THREE-LICENSE.txt.
